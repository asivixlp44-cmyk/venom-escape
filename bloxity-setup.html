Description
+1 Venom Escape: every second your symbiote grows +1 Venom, and the more Venom you have, the longer your venom tentacle gets. Jump on a glowing launch pad, release your venom, steer the tentacle across the canyon and zip along it to the next platform. Three worlds and 45 stages: the Lava Canyon with its spinning blades, sliding crushers and lava geysers, the Toxic Jungle with swinging spiked logs and snapping jaws, and the Crystal Void with sweeping lasers and rings of orbiting rocks. Each world drains your venom more, so train on the Train Zone pads to multiply your Venom, collect glowing venom orbs, fly through boost rings and keep your streak going for bigger Wins. Step on the Wins pad of every platform, land the bonus pillar for double, and walk into the golden escape portal to unlock the next world. Spend your Wins on 24 symbiotes that glow in their own colours over your outfit and tentacle, rebirth for a permanent multiplier, and climb the leaderboards with your friends. Daily rewards, playtime gifts and codes. No in-app purchases: everything is earned with Wins.
